Yes, it is completely safe. We are strictly regulated by the government and adhere to the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). Your personal information and documents are kept highly secure and confidential. We will never retain your original FIN card or passport.
4. What is the difference between a legal lender and an unlicensed one?
A legal lender is registered with the government, follows strict rules regarding interest rate caps (maximum 4% per month), and operates from a registered physical office. Unlicensed lenders, often called loan sharks, operate illegally. They charge massive, hidden interest rates, use harassment tactics, and often ask to keep your personal ID documents. Always check the official government registry to verify a lender's license.
